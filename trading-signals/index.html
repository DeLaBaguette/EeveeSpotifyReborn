How to read this

Score (0–100)
How many of nine independent conditions (trend, higher-timeframe trend, momentum, RSI, ADX, extension, volume) agree right now. A trade is only suggested at 60 or more, or 70/80 in the Strict modes. Stricter means fewer trades, and usually better ones, but never zero losers.
Risk : reward
1 : 2 means TP2 is twice as far from the entry as the stop. Bigger numbers pay more when they hit but hit less often. At 1 : 30 most trades end at TP1, at the stop or on the time exit, and only rarely at TP2. The track record shows the real numbers for your choice.
Tested on real prices
The rules were replayed on 22 months of real BTCUSD prices (January 2025 to November 2026) and compared with random trades. 15-minute and 1-hour signals were no better than random and lost money after costs, so 15-minute signals have been removed. 4-hour signals made a small profit (about +0.1R per trade at Strict, 1 : 3), and were positive in both 2025 and 2026, but there were too few trades to rule out luck. Gold had only 6 months of data, with no clear result. Treat every idea as unproven, and practise on a demo account.
Track record
The same rules, unchanged, replayed over this market’s recent history: entry on the next candle, fees and slippage included, one trade at a time, and the worst case assumed whenever a candle touched both the stop and a target.
Grade
A strong setup and a profitable record over 20+ trades. B good setup, positive record. C setup present, record thin or weak. D these signals have been losing money on this market — skip it.
R
One unit of risk — the distance from entry to stop loss. Losing a trade costs 1R. If you risk 1% per trade, +0.3R per trade on average means about +0.3% of your account per trade over time.

No grade means a trade can’t lose. Even the best setups lose a good share of the time, and losing streaks happen. You make money by only taking good setups, risking a small fixed amount (0.5–1%), and letting a positive average play out over many trades.
