How to read this

Score (0–100)
How many of nine independent conditions (trend, higher-timeframe trend, momentum, RSI, ADX, extension, volume) agree right now. A trade is only suggested at 60 or more.
Track record
The same rules, unchanged, replayed over this market’s recent history: entry on the next candle, fees and slippage included, one trade at a time, and the worst case assumed whenever a candle touched both the stop and a target.
Grade
A strong setup and a profitable record over 20+ trades. B good setup, positive record. C setup present, record thin or weak. D these signals have been losing money on this market — skip it.
R
One unit of risk — the distance from entry to stop loss. Losing a trade costs 1R. If you risk 1% per trade, +0.3R per trade on average means about +0.3% of your account per trade over time.

No grade means a trade can’t lose. Even the best setups lose a good share of the time, and losing streaks happen. You make money by only taking good setups, risking a small fixed amount (0.5–1%), and letting a positive average play out over many trades.
